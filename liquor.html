    How do I set this up? (one-time, 2 minutes)
    1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather. Send it /newbot, then choose a name and a username ending in "bot".
    2. BotFather replies with a token (a long code like 123456:ABC...). Paste it in the Bot token box.
    3. Search for your new bot in Telegram, open it, press Start and send "hi".
    4. Come back here and press Find it for me, then Send test message.
    How do I get my key?
    1. Open callmebot.com/blog/free-api-whatsapp-messages and follow the setup steps.
    2. When their WhatsApp bot is full, no new keys are issued. Use Telegram or Email instead.
    How do I get my access key? (one-time, 1 minute)
    1. Open web3forms.com, type the email address you want alerts sent to, and press Create Access Key.
    2. They email you the key. Paste it in the box above. Alerts will arrive at that same email address.
    3. Press Send test message, then check your inbox (and spam folder the first time).

    How do I get the pin from Google Maps?
    1. On a phone: open Google Maps, press and hold on the exact spot until a red pin appears, then tap the numbers at the top (or the pin name at the bottom, then Share, then Copy link). Paste it above.
    2. On a computer: open Google Maps, right-click the spot and click the numbers at the top of the menu. They are copied. Paste them above.
    3. Easiest: stand at the workplace and press Use my current location.
    4. Short links like maps.app.goo.gl cannot be read here. Open the link in Google Maps first, then copy the numbers or the long link from the address bar.
    5. Press Check it on the map after pasting to make sure the pin is on the right building.
